# 2026-11-08-replay-viewer.png, 2026-11-08-replay-viewer-fog.png: replay viewer

**What it shows:** `tools/viewer/index.html` playing a recorded game of our simulator, 4 tribes, Capitals mode,
fog of war, 16×16 map, at frame 838 of 1,485 (turn 28): Bardur's archer at 3,11 attacks the knight at 1,12
(arrow and outlines show the frame's action; the knight dies). `-viewer.png` is the true state; `-fog.png` is
the same frame viewed as Bardur, the tribe to move: tiles it can't see are dark, and other tribes' stars,
techs, city and unit totals show as "?", as in its observation (rule facts 04 §7).

**The players are uniform-random agents** (the `make bench` workload), not search: Phase 2 brings real agents.
Random play explains the road lattice and the many star gifts in the action log.

## Provenance
- Code: git `33c49d9`. Game: `make replay OBS=partial` (defaults: 4 players, Capitals, level seed 40000; the
  level is generated by Tribes' generator, then played and recorded by `tools/record.cpp`). Replay file
  `build/replays/game-4-Capitals-40000-partial.json` (366 KB, gitignored); recording it twice gives
  byte-identical files.
- Screenshots: headless Chromium (Playwright, scratch install, not a repo dependency), 1400×900 viewport,
  page loaded from `file://`, frame index 837 (0-based), "view as" all / tribe 1.

## Notes
- No game assets: a square grid with plain shapes and letters; tribe colours are the 8-slot categorical
  palette in tribe-id order. Tribe names are Tribes' enum names.
- "View as" masks tiles and the stats panel only. The viewer is not the agent's observation (e.g. it shows
  resources the tribe's techs would hide); agents only ever get `poly::observe`.
