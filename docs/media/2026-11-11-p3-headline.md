## Interpretation and caveats
- **Macro vs. flat.** Macro's point estimate is higher in all 4 shared cells, but each pair of intervals
  overlaps, so this table alone doesn't separate them. The head-to-head evidence is SPRT 3 (`macro=1` vs.
  `macro=0`, dev levels, Fog): H1 after 200 pairs, Elo +47.2 (95% +19.8 to +75.2), ADR-008 §8. Flat PUCT vs.
  Rule Based in Paper (55.5%, 48.6–62.2) is not significant on its own.
- **Fog vs. Paper.** PUCT wins more often under fog against the search baselines (RHEA 91.0% vs. 77.5%, MCTS
  94.0% vs. 75.0%; intervals disjoint). Not explained yet; one candidate is that those baselines' rollouts suffer
  more from Tribes' fogged observation than PUCT's determinized search does. Untested. Where the gap comes from
  (same CSVs): conquests. PUCT's conquest wins/losses go from 120/6 (Paper) to 154/4 (Fog) vs. RHEA and 111/11
  to 156/0 vs. MCTS, while vs. Rule Based they stay level (94/40, 91/37). Games reaching the 50-tick cap are
  decided on score and are close to even in Paper against RHEA (35/39) and MCTS (39/39).
- **Rule Based is the hardest opponent** (65.5% / 67.5%), as it was in Phase 2's round robin.
- **Illegal actions.** 29 returned actions were illegal (no-ops), in 3 cells, only in games with RHEA in them
  (as in Phase 2: Tribes' RHEA behaviour). The CSV counts them per game, not per seat; PUCT chooses from the
  legal set by construction.
- **Timing.** The last cell took 3.5 h of wall time for 4,432 summed game-seconds (max game 118 s): the machine
  was presumably suspended. Per-game timings are unaffected; results don't depend on time (fixed FM budget).
