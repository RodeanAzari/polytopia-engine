## Macro-actions (ADR-010, d4ae3c5)
Measured first (scratch tools, RB vs RB, fog, 100 dev levels): moves are 62% of legal actions and 66% of
PUCT's prior mass; 23 actions tie at RB's top score per decision. A 2,000-call flat tree's principal line
covers ~2.9 of the ~12 decisions of our own turn. User approved the design: per unit one move per goal
(attack, siege, capture, explore, defend, RB's pick), Destroy/Disband pruned, Send stars max per target.
Same probe with macro=1: root children 62.7 → 22.1, depth 7.8 → 10.5, reaches opponent's turn 55% → 84%.

Bug: macro PUCT aborted at its first inner expansion ("agent_advance: list isn't s's legal set").
Root cause: expand() passed a node's edges as the copy's own legal set; with macros the edges are a subset.
Fix: macro nodes also keep their full legal set (pool, reused), not regenerated (legal generation is the
profile's biggest cost).

Testing lessons (bug injection, 16 caught in the end):
- Capitals start with a warrior: the enemy unit on (8, 8) makes our capital a defend target in "empty"
  positions. Two hand-built tests were wrong until positions were tabulated per goal.
- A position must make each goal's move unique, or dropping a goal goes unnoticed (first attack/siege test).
- A crash isn't a "[  FAILED  ]" line: the injection harness first reported an aborting mutant as missed.
- Symmetric positions hide perspective bugs (macro from the searcher's view instead of the mover's): our
  defend = their siege when each side has one city. A unit off the diagonal exposed it.
- Equivalent mutant: dropping `seen()` on enemy units (observations already omit hidden units).
